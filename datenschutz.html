2. Summary

The game works without a user account and without signing in. No analytics or tracking services are used and no data is transmitted to me or to third parties.

3. Locally stored data

To keep your progress, the game stores a save game in the local storage of your device: points, purchased upgrades, skins, records, statistics, missions, achievements, the daily challenge and settings (e.g. sound on/off). This data does not leave your device and contains no information about you personally.

The legal basis is § 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG (the storage is strictly necessary for the game you requested). You can delete the data at any time by clearing the app data in the Android settings or by uninstalling the app – your game progress will be lost in the process.

4. Permissions

Vibration: for brief haptic feedback during the game.
Internet: required by the app runtime (Capacitor). The game itself does not connect to any servers; all graphics, sounds and fonts are included in the app.

5. Google Play

If you download the game via Google Play, Google processes data (e.g. account and device data) under its own responsibility. I have no influence on this. More information: policies.google.com/privacy

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. For questions about data protection you can reach me at AMpireGames@gmail.com.

7. Changes

This privacy policy will be updated if the game or the legal situation changes. The version shown in the app applies.
